01 / About

About Me

I am an honors aerospace engineering student driven by a passion for solving complex engineering problems through a combination of analytical thinking and hands-on development. I enjoy working on multidisciplinary systems that bring together mechanics, aerodynamics, structures, controls, and manufacturing, and I am especially motivated by environments where engineering ideas quickly become physical hardware. My interests extend across aerospace and advanced mechanical systems, with a strong focus on building high-performance technologies that operate in demanding real-world conditions.

My experience includes propulsion system modeling, CFD research, aerodynamic design, aerospace structures, and large-scale hardware integration through internships, research laboratories, and engineering organizations. I have developed propulsion analysis tools, performed turbulent flow simulations on supercomputing clusters, designed aerodynamic and structural components in CAD, conducted FEA and CFD analyses, and supported manufacturing and integration workflows for complex mechanical systems. Through organizations such as Texas Spacecraft Laboratory and Longhorn Racing Electric, as well as internships at The Boring Company and Flamefront Propulsion, I have gained experience working across the full engineering process from concept development and simulation to fabrication, testing, and validation.

I am pursuing opportunities to support cutting-edge system development and engineering innovation across aerospace, mechanical, and advanced technology domains. I am particularly interested in roles that combine technical analysis with hands-on engineering, rapid design iteration, and real-world system integration. My goal is to continue developing as an engineer by working on challenging projects that push the boundaries of performance, reliability, and manufacturability while collaborating with highly driven engineering teams.
